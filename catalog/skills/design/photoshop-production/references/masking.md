# Masking and background removal

Choose the method from the edge, not from habit:

- Device Select Subject for people, products, and mixed photographic edges;
- pen paths or shape layers for exact hard products and architecture;
- channels/calculations for hair, glass, smoke, pale objects, or low-contrast separation;
- known-color key for controlled chroma backgrounds;
- manual repair when automation removes required foreground.

Keep the original pixel layer, the raw selection or mask, and the corrected mask separately. Do not destructively erase production pixels.

Inspect the alpha on white, black, 50% gray, and the intended background. Check fringe color, missing pale regions, semi-transparent solid surfaces, pinholes, internal holes, thin cables/hair, stair-stepping, and dirty RGB in transparent pixels. A transparent PNG alone is not the editable master.
