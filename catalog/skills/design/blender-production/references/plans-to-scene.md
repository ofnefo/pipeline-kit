# Plans and schemes to a Blender scene

## Source order

Prefer measured CAD/BIM or dimensioned drawings, then verified PDF plans/elevations/sections, then site measurements, then photographs for cross-checking. Perspective images and conceptual renders are not dimensional authority.

Before modeling, establish units, one known scale, origin, north/front direction, floor levels, ceiling/rigging heights, wall thickness assumptions, and the coordinate relationship between plan, elevations, and sections. If sources disagree, preserve both claims and stop the affected dimension at `TBD`.

Build in passes:

1. reference planes and scale checks;
2. floors, walls, openings, columns, stairs, lifts, and fixed venue geometry;
3. circulation, service access, equipment envelopes, and sightline blockers;
4. project objects and modular assemblies;
5. validation views: top, key elevations, sections, and one clay camera.

Compare known distances and level heights numerically after import. Do not use a perspective screenshot as a tracing plane without camera calibration and explicit uncertainty.
